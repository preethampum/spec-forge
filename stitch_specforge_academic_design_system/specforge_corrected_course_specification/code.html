Course Description

This course provides an advanced treatment of algorithmic design, computational complexity, and intractable optimization. Students investigate non-deterministic polynomial reductions, randomized protocols, amortized data structural bounds, and streaming models. Rigorous mathematical proof techniques and real-world system abstractions are emphasized throughout problem sets and research case evaluations.

4. Course Contents (Units / Modules with Topics and Hours)

auto_awesome Edited by AI

Unit 1: Divide & Conquer, Recurrences & Amortized Analysis

8 Contact Hours
  • Master Theorem cases, substitution method, and recursion tree transformations.
  • Amortized Accounting & Potential methods: Dynamic array resizing and binary counter bounds.
  • Advanced persistent search trees and splay tree amortized bounds.

Unit 2: Graph Algorithms & Network Flow Reductions

10 Contact Hours
  • Max-Flow Min-Cut Theorem, Ford-Fulkerson method, and Edmonds-Karp complexity bounds.
  • Push-Relabel algorithm with highest-label selection heuristic.
  • Applications: Maximum bipartite matching, minimum path cover in DAGs, and project scheduling.

Unit 3: Randomized Algorithms & Probabilistic Analysis

8 Contact Hours
  • Monte Carlo vs Las Vegas algorithms; Randomized QuickSort expected depth bounds.
  • Karger’s minimum cut algorithm and contraction analysis.
  • Chernoff bounds, Markov & Chebyshev inequalities applied to hash universal families.

Unit 4: NP-Completeness & Intractability Proofs

AI Adjusted
9 Contact Hours
  • Classes P, NP, NP-Hard, and NP-Complete; Cook-Levin Theorem foundations.
  • Polynomial-time Karp reductions: 3-SAT to Clique, Vertex Cover, and Subset-Sum.
  • Dealing with hardness: Branch-and-bound and fixed-parameter tractability (FPT).
info AI note: Added formal reduction techniques from Garey & Johnson standard for Tier-1 outcome compliance.

Unit 5: Approximation Algorithms & Heuristics

7 Contact Hours
  • Approximation ratios and schemes: PTAS and FPTAS definitions.
  • Metric TSP: 2-approximation using MST and 1.5-approximation Christofides algorithm.
  • Set Cover greedy logarithmic approximation; Knapsack dynamic programming FPTAS.
